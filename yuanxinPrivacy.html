This Mini Game is intended for users outside Mainland China and is not offered to users located within Mainland China.

What information does the Mini Game obtain and how is it used?

The Mini Game does not require user registration. We rely on the official TikTok Minis SDK for user authentication. We do not collect or persistently store any TikTok account identity information on our servers. All user personal identity data is processed and maintained by TikTok, subject to TikTok’s Privacy Policy.

What information does the Mini Game collect automatically?

The Mini Game may automatically collect anonymous game usage data, including game interaction records, device type, device operating system, and anonymous performance statistics for game optimization. We do not obtain precise real-time location data.

Does the Mini Game collect precise real time location information of the device?

This Mini Game does not gather precise location information of your mobile device.

We serve advertisements through the built-in advertising capability of TikTok Minis. Ad-related data processing is governed by TikTok’s own privacy policy. Aggregated and anonymized game statistics may be shared to improve game experience and ad performance.

Children’s Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children below the minimum age required under applicable local laws. If you believe we have inadvertently received data from an underage user, please contact us promptly for removal.

What are your rights?

You may stop the Mini Game from collecting anonymous game usage data by closing the Mini Game in TikTok. If you wish to exercise your data rights related to personal information held by TikTok, please contact TikTok directly; for inquiries about anonymized game data processed by us, you may contact us via email below. Nothing in this policy limits any non‑waivable statutory rights you hold under the privacy laws of your country of residence.

What is the data retention policy and how is data secured?

We do not persistently store any TikTok user personal data. Any temporarily cached non‑personal runtime data will be protected with industry‑standard encryption and cleared after the game session ends.

Anonymous game statistics will be retained for up to 24 months and then stored only in aggregated, anonymized form.
