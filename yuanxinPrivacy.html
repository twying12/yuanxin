Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to our Mini Game (hereinafter referred to as “Mini Game”) developed by Huizhou Yuanxin Culture Communication Co., Ltd. (hereinafter referred to as “Service Provider”). This Mini Game is an ad-supported service provided “AS IS” within the TikTok Minis ecosystem.

What information does the Mini Game obtain and how is it used?

The Mini Game does not require user registration. We do not collect or store TikTok account identity information persistently.

What information does the Mini Game collect automatically?

The Mini Game may automatically collect anonymous game usage data, including game interaction records, device type, device operating system, and anonymous performance statistics for game optimization. We do not obtain precise real-time location data.

Does the Mini Game collect precise real time location information of the device?

This Mini Game does not gather precise location information of your mobile device.

Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Mini Game?

We serve advertisements through the built-in advertising capability of TikTok Minis. Ad-related data processing is governed by TikTok’s own privacy policy. Aggregated and anonymized game statistics may be shared to improve game experience and ad performance.

The Service Provider may disclose information:

as required by law, such as to comply with a subpoena, or similar legal process;

when we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, protect your safety or the safety of others, investigate fraud, or respond to a government request;

with our trusted service providers acting on our behalf, who have agreed to adhere to the rules set forth in this privacy statement.

What are your rights?

You may stop the Mini Game from collecting data by closing the Mini Game in TikTok. If you wish to exercise your data rights, please contact us via email below.

What is the data retention policy and how is data secured?

We host our service and store business data in Mainland China. Our team accesses and processes data from Mainland China. We do not persistently store TikTok user personal data. Any temporarily cached data will be protected with industry-standard encryption.

Anonymous game statistics will be retained for up to 24 months and then stored only in aggregated, anonymized form.
